Markers / distance / angle

Two independent tools for annotating and measuring your images: a distance/angle ruler, and persistent point markers.

Open via Plot → Markers / distance / angle (Ctrl+Shift+T).

Measuring distance and angle

Click Draw line and draw across the two points you want to measure. The panel reports Delta x / Delta y, Length and the angle to the horizontal and to the vertical, in calibrated units once a calibration is set.

Placing markers

Click Set markers, then left-click each point you want to mark; right-click to finish. Display markers and Clear markers control them afterwards.

Markers can outlive a session Enable Hold markers to keep your markers even after starting a new session — they're only cleared when you restart PIVlab itself.

The Display average button shows the mean image across all loaded frames — handy for placing markers on a feature that's clearer in the time-averaged view than in any single frame.
