Synthetic particle image generation

Generates synthetic particle image pairs from a known flow field, with the true displacements in ground_truth.mat — for testing PIV settings against the correct answer, or for learning how PIV parameters affect the result without real data.

Open via Synthetic particle image generation → Particle image generator (Ctrl+G).

Setup

SetupWhat you get
Simple: one camera straight onThe camera looks straight at the light sheet: no perspective, no lens distortion, nothing to calibrate. You set the image size and the pixel size in the sheet (mm per pixel; displacements in mm divided by it are displacements in px). The image pairs are loaded into PIVlab after generating.
One camera with lensA camera with position, aim point, focal length and lens distortion, plus images of the ChArUco board (light sheet position and calibration images) — to test the camera calibration and the rectification.
Two cameras (stereo-PIV)As above for two cameras; the light sheet can be offset and tilted against the board, so the disparity correction has to find it. See Stereo-PIV. Show 3D preview shows the board, the sheet, the cameras and what they see; drag the cameras with the mouse.

The settings are on two pages (Settings): camera and light sheet, and flow, particles and output. The ChArUco board is the one of Camera calibration → Setup / define / generate marker board.

Flow

The flow is centred in the view (with two cameras: in the overlap of both views). The displacements are given in mm per image pair.

FlowParameters
Rankine vortex, Hamel-Oseen vortexMaximum displacement, core radius, vortex pair distance (0 = one vortex, > 0 = two counter-rotating vortices side by side), out-of-plane displacement.
Vortex with out-of-plane jetA Rankine vortex with a jet through its core (peak out-of-plane displacement) — a test case for stereo-PIV.
Uniform displacement, solid body rotation, membraneMaximum displacement, out-of-plane displacement.
Sphere wake, free jet (3D flow simulation)An unsteady 3D flow is computed (1 to 3 minutes): the wake of a sphere in the light sheet, or a round jet from a nozzle at the left edge of the view (Re 3000; it behaves like a real round jet: potential core about 5 diameters, spreading about 0.09). Parameters: free stream / jet displacement, diameter of the sphere / nozzle, and the time between pairs in D/U (the flow travels this many diameters between two image pairs). Every image pair is a later moment of the flow: a time series. The view must end within 10 jet diameters of the nozzle.

Generating and using the images

  1. Click Generate images... and select an output folder.
  2. Simple setup: the image pairs are loaded into PIVlab — analyze them and compare with ground_truth.mat: truth.X_px, Y_px, U_px, V_px are the true positions and displacements in px (sphere and jet: one page per image pair).
  3. Camera setups: README.txt in the folder describes the files and the workflow (lens calibration, rectification, stereo-PIV, comparison with the ground truth).
