Keyboard shortcuts

Every shortcut jumps straight to a panel — the same panel you'd reach by clicking the matching menu item.

Images and sessions

ShortcutOpens

Ctrl+Shift+OImport stereo images (camera 1 and camera 2)

Image settings

ShortcutOpens

Analysis and validation

Stereo-PIV cameras

ShortcutDoes

Ctrl+Shift+2Show and edit camera 2

Same as the CAM 1 / CAM 2 switch in the Tools panel — see Stereo-PIV.
