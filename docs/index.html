PIVlab – free (stereo) particle image velocimetry software

PIVlab is free and open-source particle image velocimetry (PIV) software, and currently the most frequently cited PIV tool on the market. It calculates the velocity distribution in imported or captured particle images, derives, displays and exports many parameters of the flow, and can control OPTOLUTION's lasers, cameras and synchronizers. The graphical user interface makes PIV data acquisition and post-processing fast and efficient.
