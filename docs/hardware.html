Hardware

A range of hardware has been developed for PIVlab, so PIVlab can also control pulsed light sources (lasers, LEDs), grab images from cameras, and control seeding generators and camera lenses. The videos below give an overview of these features. I developed this hardware with OPTOLUTION Messtechnik GmbH, the company I am working for.

Everything is operated from within PIVlab: you set up the laser and the synchronizer, see the live camera image, record the PIV images and analyse them in one program – with one camera, or with two cameras for stereo-PIV. How to record is explained in the manual: Capturing images from a camera.
