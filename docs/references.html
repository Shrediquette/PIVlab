Journal papers etc. that used PIVlab

I am really happy to see that a huge number of studies (> 3000) use PIVlab for all kinds of experiments: fluid dynamics, sand and gravel motion, cell motion, blood flow, flow mapping of rivers, 3D printing, dam breaching and more. Most of the citations can be found on Google Scholar:
