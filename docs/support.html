PIVlab Support

You can get support in the Google Groups forum. Usually I am very responsive. If you have problems analyzing your data, please always provide the original data in the forum. And if I have a fix or a solution for your problem, please give me feedback if it worked!

Many questions are also answered in the user manual, for example on the best practices & troubleshooting page.

Service offers

In addition to self-made PIV hardware, OPTOLUTION offers a wide range of services in the flow measurement field with state-of-the-art LDV and PIV equipment and more than a decade of experience. More information and examples are on the OPTOLUTION service offers page.
