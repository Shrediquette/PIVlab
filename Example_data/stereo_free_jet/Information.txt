PIVlab synthetic stereo-PIV recording (particle image generator, two cameras), 25-Oct-2026
A free round jet (3D flow simulation, nozzle diameter 40 mm, Re 3000) seen by two cameras at 30 degrees to
the light sheet normal, on both sides of the jet (60 degrees between the cameras), lens distortion on both
cameras. The light sheet is 0.5 mm in front of the ChArUco board and tilted by 1 degree, so the disparity
correction has to find it. 5 image pairs per camera, 0.5 D/U apart (a time series of the jet);
displacement at the nozzle exit 1.6 mm (about 7 px). The images are jpg compressed.

Files per camera (k = 1, 2):
* camera<k>/particle_images: the particle image pairs (cam<k>_0001_A.jpg, cam<k>_0001_B.jpg, ...)
* camera<k>/calibration_images: the board in random positions, for the lens calibration
* camera<k>/board_in_light_sheet_cam<k>.jpg: the ChArUco board where the light sheet is
* camera<k>/lens_cam<k>.mat: the true lens of the camera ("Load camera parameters")

ChArUco board: 10 x 18 squares, 12 mm squares, 9 mm markers, DICT_4X4_1000, black origin checker
(Camera calibration -> Setup / define / generate marker board).

Workflow in PIVlab:
* Import stereo images: the particle images of camera 1 and of camera 2
* For camera 1 and camera 2 (switch with the CAM 1 / CAM 2 button at the bottom left):
* Image settings -> Camera calibration -> Camera calibration (undistortion):
  load the calibration images and estimate the camera parameters (or load lens_cam<k>.mat)
* Image settings -> Camera calibration -> Image rectification / alignment:
  load board_in_light_sheet_cam<k>.jpg as target image
* Analysis -> Stereo-PIV: compute the stereo calibration, measure the disparity, analyze

Command line: Example_scripts/PIVlab_api_stereo.m uses this recording.
